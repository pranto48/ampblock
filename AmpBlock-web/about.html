OUR MISSION

Restoring Freedom, Speed, & Dignity to the Open Web

The internet was conceived as an open repository of human knowledge. Over the past decade, it has been suffocated by invasive surveillance trackers, unskippable video ads, and deceptive click-traps.

Why AmpBlock Was Created

When Chromium announced the deprecation of Manifest V2, many feared effective ad blocking was dead. Ad tech corporations hoped extensions would be hobbled.

AmpBlock was engineered from the ground up to prove that Manifest V3 can be even faster, more battery-friendly, and completely uncompromising when combined with surgical DOM manipulation and declarative kernel filtering.

Our Ironclad Privacy Manifesto

Unlike commercial ad blockers that monetize by selling "Acceptable Ads" programs or routing user traffic through proprietary proxies:

  • Zero Remote Servers: AmpBlock has no central database, no analytics collection, and no telemetry pings.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not know what websites you visit, how long you browse, or who you are.
  • 100% Local Execution: All whitelist rules, custom filters, and block counts live exclusively in your local chrome.storage.local.

100% Free & Open-Source Software (FOSS)

We believe high-speed, ad-free internet is a basic digital right. AmpBlock is 100% free and open-source forever.

There are no paid tiers, no premium paywalls, no trial periods, and no tracking. Anyone—from casual internet users to developers and cybersecurity researchers—can download the source code, inspect every line of JavaScript, audit our declarative filtering rules, and use or fork it completely free of charge.
